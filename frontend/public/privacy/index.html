Spendly Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Information Spendly Uses

Spendly connects to your Google account with your permission to access Gmail data needed to identify supported financial transaction alerts.

How Your Information Is Used

Gmail data is used to identify transaction information such as merchant, amount, date, and card information contained in supported bank alerts. This information is used to calculate and display your Safe-to-Spend balance.

Data Storage

Spendly stores transaction information required to provide its budgeting and Safe-to-Spend features. Google authentication credentials are used only to maintain the Gmail connection authorized by the user.

Data Sharing

Spendly does not sell your personal information or Gmail data.

Access and Revocation

You may revoke Spendly's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to the support email listed for Spendly.
